# Getting Started

When you open the app you land on the connection screen, with two tabs: **Join Server** and **My Servers**.

Before anything else, pick your **nickname** and, if you want, a **profile picture**. These details are saved on your PC and follow you into the servers you join.

## Pick your path

- 👥 A friend already has a server: see [Join a Server](/en/entrar-em-um-servidor).
- 🏠 You want to host: see [Create Your Server](/en/criar-seu-servidor).
- 🖧 You want a 24/7 Linux/VPS server: see [Host on a VPS](/en/hospedar-em-vps).
