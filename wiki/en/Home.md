# Monky Wiki

Monky is a desktop app for voice, video, screen sharing and chat with your friends — on your own server, with no sign-up and no middlemen.

## How it works

1. One person hosts from the app or on a VPS.
2. Friends join by entering the server IP and port.
3. Voice, video and screen sharing travel P2P via WebRTC; the server handles login, channels, chat and signalling.
